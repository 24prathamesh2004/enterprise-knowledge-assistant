# Benefits Policy

Policy owner: Benefits, People Ops (Laura Bianchi, laura.bianchi@northwind.example)
Effective date: 2026-01-01 | Version 4.1

## Overview

Northwind Technologies offers a comprehensive benefits package to support the health, financial security, and wellbeing of employees and their families. This policy summarizes the core benefits available to full-time employees.

## Enrollment Window

New employees must complete benefits enrollment **within 30 days** of their start date through the People Portal. Employees who miss this window must wait until the annual open enrollment period (each November) unless they experience a qualifying life event (marriage, birth, or loss of other coverage), which opens a **30-day special enrollment** window.

## Retirement — 401(k)

Northwind matches employee 401(k) contributions at **100% of the first 5%** of eligible salary contributed. Employer matching contributions are **immediately vested**. Employees may enroll or change contribution levels at any time. Eligibility begins on the first of the month following the start date.

## Health Insurance

Medical, dental, and vision coverage becomes effective on the **first day of the month following** the start date. Northwind pays **80% of the medical premium** for employees and 60% for dependents. Employees choose between a PPO and a High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with an HSA option.

### Health Savings Account

For employees on the HDHP, Northwind contributes **$750 per year** to an individual HSA and $1,500 for family coverage.

## Wellness Stipend

All full-time employees receive an annual **wellness stipend of $600**, reimbursable for gym memberships, fitness equipment, or mental-health services. The stipend resets each January and is claimed through the Expense Portal.

## Paid Time Off

Paid leave, sick days, and parental leave are governed by the separate Leave Policy. Benefits and leave are administered together by People Ops.

## Life and Disability Insurance

- **Basic life insurance:** equal to 2x annual base salary, fully company-paid.
- **Short-term disability:** covers 60% of salary for up to 12 weeks.
- **Long-term disability:** covers 50% of salary after 12 weeks.

Employees may purchase supplemental life coverage during enrollment.

## Employee Assistance Program

The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offers free, confidential counseling — up to **8 sessions per issue per year** — plus legal and financial consultations. The EAP is available 24/7 to employees and household members.

## Tuition Assistance

Northwind reimburses up to **$5,000 per year** in approved tuition or professional certification costs, subject to manager approval and a passing grade. Employees must remain with Northwind for 12 months after reimbursement or repay a prorated amount.

## Questions

For benefits questions, contact the Benefits team at benefits@northwind.example.
