# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Policy owner: Finance (Priya Nair, priya.nair@northwind.example)
Effective date: 2026-01-15 | Version 4.0

## Purpose

This policy explains how Northwind Technologies employees submit, approve, and receive reimbursement for business expenses incurred on behalf of the company.

## Submission Deadline

All expense claims must be submitted through the Expense Portal **within 30 days** of the date the expense was incurred. Claims submitted after 30 days require Director approval and may be declined. Claims older than **90 days** will not be reimbursed under any circumstances.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expenses are reimbursed via payroll or direct deposit **within 14 business days** of final approval. Employees are notified by email once payment is scheduled.

## Receipts and Documentation

An itemized receipt is required for any single expense **over $25**. For expenses of $25 or less, a Portal entry describing the purpose is sufficient. Credit card statements alone are not accepted as receipts. Each claim must include a business justification and the associated project or cost center.

## Approval Thresholds

Approval requirements scale with the claim total:

- **Up to $500:** direct manager approval.
- **$501 to $2,000:** direct manager plus Director approval.
- **Over $2,000:** Director plus Finance approval.

Employees may not approve their own expenses, and no employee may approve the expenses of someone at or above their own level.

## Meals and Entertainment

- **Domestic meals:** capped at **$75 per day** per employee.
- **Client entertainment:** allowed with prior manager approval; attendee names and business purpose must be recorded.
- **Alcohol:** reimbursable only as part of a client meal, up to 2 drinks per person.

Note: while traveling, the per-diem rates in the Travel Policy apply instead of the domestic meal cap.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are never reimbursed:

- Personal entertainment, fines, or traffic violations.
- Airline seat upgrades beyond policy (see Travel Policy).
- Home internet beyond the remote-work stipend.
- Expenses lacking a valid receipt where one is required.

## Corporate Cards

Employees who travel frequently may be issued a corporate card. Corporate card transactions must still be reconciled in the Expense Portal within **30 days**. Failure to reconcile within 60 days may result in card suspension.

## Mileage

Personal vehicle use for business is reimbursed at **$0.62 per mile**, calculated from the standard route. Commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable.

## Disputes

If a claim is declined, employees may appeal to Finance within 10 business days at finance@northwind.example. Finance will respond within 5 business days with a final decision.
