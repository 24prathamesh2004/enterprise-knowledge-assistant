# Leave Policy

Policy owner: People Ops (Maria Alvarez, maria.alvarez@northwind.example)
Effective date: 2026-01-01 | Version 3.1

## Purpose and Scope

This policy defines how Northwind Technologies employees request, accrue, and use paid time off. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees on the Northwind payroll. Contractors and interns are governed by their individual agreements and are not covered here.

## Annual Paid Leave

Full-time employees are entitled to **22 days of paid annual leave** per calendar year, accrued at a rate of 1.83 days per completed month of service. Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rata basis proportional to their contracted hours.

Employees must submit planned annual leave requests at least **14 calendar days in advance** through the People Portal. Requests are approved by the employee's direct manager, and managers should respond within 3 business days.

### Carryover and Payout

A maximum of **5 unused annual leave days** may be carried over into the following calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by **March 31**, after which they expire. Unused annual leave is not paid out except upon termination of employment, where accrued but unused days are settled in the final paycheck.

## Sick Leave

Employees receive **10 paid sick days** per calendar year, separate from annual leave. Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out. A medical certificate is required for any sick absence exceeding **3 consecutive working days**. Employees should notify their manager before 10:00 AM local time on the first day of absence.

## Parental Leave

Northwind offers **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave** to the primary caregiver and **4 weeks of fully paid leave** to the secondary caregiver. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption of a child. Employees must provide at least **30 days written notice** before the intended start date, where reasonably practicable.

## Bereavement and Other Leave

- **Bereavement leave:** up to 5 paid days for an immediate family member.
- **Jury duty:** paid for the full duration of service.
- **Unpaid personal leave:** may be granted at management discretion for up to 20 working days per year.

## Public Holidays

Northwind observes **11 public holidays** per year, published each January in the People Portal. Public holidays that fall on a weekend are observed on the following Monday.

## Requesting and Approving Leave

All leave is requested through the People Portal. The approval chain is: employee request → direct manager approval → automatic People Ops record. If a manager does not respond within 3 business days, the request is escalated to the department head. Emergency leave may be taken with retroactive submission within 2 business days of returning to work.
