# Data Privacy Policy

Policy owner: Data Protection Office (Nadia Hassan, data-privacy@northwind.example)
Effective date: 2026-03-01 | Version 2.2

## Purpose and Scope

This policy describes how Northwind Technologies collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data belonging to customers, employees, and partners. It applies to all employees and systems that process personal data on Northwind's behalf.

## Principles

Northwind processes personal data lawfully, fairly, and transparently. Data is collected only for specified, legitimate purposes and limited to what is necessary. We keep personal data accurate and up to date and protect it with appropriate security controls.

## Data Retention

Personal data is retained only as long as necessary:

- **Customer account data:** retained for **7 years** after account closure.
- **Employee records:** retained for **6 years** after employment ends.
- **Marketing consent records:** retained for **3 years** after the last interaction.
- **Website analytics:** anonymized after **26 months**.

When a retention period expires, data is securely deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

## Data Subject Rights

Individuals may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal data. Northwind fulfills verified data-subject requests **within 30 days** of receipt. Requests are submitted to privacy@northwind.example and logged by the Data Protection Office.

## Security Controls

- Personal data is encrypted at rest using **AES-256** and in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher.
- Access to personal data is role-based and reviewed quarterly.
- Confidential and Restricted data may be accessed only on company-issued devices, per the IT Security Policy.

## Breach Notification

In the event of a personal-data breach, the Data Protection Office must notify the relevant supervisory authority **within 72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach. Affected individuals are notified without undue delay where there is a high risk to their rights. All employees must report a suspected breach to security@northwind.example **within 1 hour** of discovery.

## Third Parties and Data Sharing

Personal data is shared with third-party processors only under a signed Data Processing Agreement. Vendors handling personal data are assessed before onboarding and audited annually. International data transfers use approved safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Employee Responsibilities

Employees must:

- Handle personal data only for legitimate business purposes.
- Never store personal data in unauthorized locations or personal accounts.
- Complete Data Privacy training within their first 30 days and annually thereafter.
- Report any suspected mishandling of data immediately.

## Cookies and Tracking

The Northwind website uses only essential cookies by default. Non-essential cookies require explicit opt-in consent, and users may withdraw consent at any time.

## Governance

The Data Protection Office oversees compliance, maintains the record of processing activities, and conducts Data Protection Impact Assessments for high-risk processing. Questions should be directed to data-privacy@northwind.example.
