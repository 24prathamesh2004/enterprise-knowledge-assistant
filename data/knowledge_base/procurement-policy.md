# Procurement Policy

Policy owner: Procurement (George Miller, george.miller@northwind.example)
Effective date: 2026-01-10 | Version 3.5

## Purpose

This policy governs how Northwind Technologies purchases goods and services, ensuring spending is controlled, competitive, and properly authorized. It applies to all employees who initiate or approve purchases.

## Approval Thresholds

All purchases require approval before a commitment is made to a vendor. Approval authority scales with the purchase amount:

- **Under $1,000:** direct manager approval.
- **$1,000 to $5,000:** Director approval.
- **Over $5,000:** **VP approval** required.
- **Over $50,000:** VP plus CFO approval.

Splitting a purchase into smaller orders to avoid a higher approval tier is prohibited.

## Competitive Bidding

To ensure value for money:

- Purchases **over $10,000** require at least **three written vendor quotes**.
- Purchases over $50,000 require a formal RFP managed by the Procurement team.
- Sole-source purchases must include a written justification approved by a VP.

## Preferred Vendors

Northwind maintains a list of preferred vendors with negotiated rates. Employees should use preferred vendors where available. Onboarding a new vendor takes approximately **15 business days** and requires a completed vendor questionnaire, tax documentation, and a signed Data Processing Agreement where personal data is involved.

## Purchase Orders

A purchase order (PO) must be raised in the Procurement Portal for all purchases **over $1,000** before goods or services are ordered. Invoices without a matching PO may be rejected by Finance. POs are matched against invoices and delivery confirmations before payment.

## Payment Terms

Standard payment terms are **Net 30** from the invoice date. Early-payment discounts should be captured where offered. Vendors are paid via the scheduled accounts-payable run twice monthly.

## Contracts

Any contract with a term longer than **12 months** or a total value over $25,000 must be reviewed by Legal & Compliance before signature. Only authorized signatories may execute contracts on behalf of Northwind; individual employees may not sign vendor agreements without delegated authority.

## Corporate Card Purchases

Low-value purchases under $1,000 may be made on a corporate card where a PO is not practical, subject to manager approval and reconciliation in the Expense Portal within 30 days.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees involved in purchasing must disclose any personal or financial relationship with a vendor, per the Code of Conduct. Such employees must recuse themselves from the related vendor selection.

## Sustainability

Procurement favors vendors with strong environmental and labor standards. Where price and quality are comparable, preference is given to certified sustainable suppliers.

## Questions

Contact the Procurement team at procurement@northwind.example for vendor onboarding, PO support, or policy exceptions.
