# Code of Conduct

Policy owner: Legal & Compliance (Rachel Kim, rachel.kim@northwind.example)
Effective date: 2026-01-01 | Version 2.0

## Our Commitment

Northwind Technologies is committed to a workplace built on integrity, respect, and accountability. This Code applies to all employees, officers, contractors, and board members. Every individual is responsible for reading, understanding, and following it.

## Respectful Workplace

Northwind prohibits harassment, discrimination, bullying, and retaliation of any kind. We value diversity and expect all interactions — in person and online — to be professional and inclusive. Discrimination on the basis of race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any protected characteristic is strictly forbidden.

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with those of Northwind. Any potential conflict — such as a financial interest in a vendor or a close relationship with a direct report — must be disclosed to Legal & Compliance in writing within **10 business days** of becoming aware of it.

## Gifts and Entertainment

Employees may accept modest business gifts, but any gift or entertainment with a value **over $100** must be disclosed to Legal & Compliance and recorded in the Gifts Register. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts of any amount are never permitted. Gifts to government officials are prohibited without prior Legal approval.

## Anti-Bribery and Corruption

Northwind has a zero-tolerance stance on bribery and corruption. Employees must never offer, promise, or accept anything of value to improperly influence a business decision. This applies to all third parties acting on Northwind's behalf.

## Confidentiality

Employees must protect Northwind's confidential and proprietary information, as well as that of customers and partners, both during and after employment. Confidential information must not be disclosed without authorization.

## Use of Company Resources

Company assets — including devices, funds, and time — are to be used for legitimate business purposes. Incidental personal use of devices is permitted if it does not interfere with work or violate the IT Security Policy.

## Reporting Violations

Employees who witness or suspect a violation must report it. Reports can be made to a manager, to Legal & Compliance at ethics@northwind.example, or anonymously through the 24/7 Ethics Hotline. Northwind investigates all good-faith reports and aims to complete each investigation **within 30 days**.

### Non-Retaliation

Northwind strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation. Retaliation is itself a serious violation of this Code and will be disciplined accordingly.

## Consequences

Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment and, where applicable, referral to law enforcement.

## Acknowledgment

All employees must acknowledge this Code annually through the People Portal. Questions may be directed to Legal & Compliance at compliance@northwind.example.
