# Onboarding Guide

Policy owner: People Ops (Tomás Rivera, tomas.rivera@northwind.example)
Effective date: 2026-01-01 | Version 3.0

## Welcome to Northwind Technologies

This guide walks new hires through their first weeks at Northwind. Our goal is to make sure every new employee feels supported, has the tools they need, and understands how we work.

## Before Day One

Your hiring manager and People Ops will send a welcome email confirming your start date, location, and first-day schedule. IT ships a company laptop to arrive **before your start date**. Complete your pre-boarding paperwork in the People Portal at least **3 business days before** you begin.

## Your First Day

New hires begin at **9:30 AM** on their start date. The first day includes:

- A welcome session with People Ops.
- Account setup verification with IT.
- Introduction to your team and assigned onboarding buddy.
- A review of workplace tools and communication norms.

### IT Setup

IT completes full account and device setup **within 2 business days** of your start date. This includes email, MFA enrollment, VPN access, and role-based system access. If anything is missing after 2 days, raise a ticket in the Service Portal.

## Onboarding Buddy

Every new hire is paired with an **onboarding buddy** from their team for the first 30 days. The buddy answers day-to-day questions, helps with introductions, and supports cultural integration. Buddies are not managers and do not conduct performance reviews.

## Mandatory Training

All new employees must complete the mandatory training modules **within the first 30 days**, including:

- IT Security and Acceptable Use.
- Code of Conduct and Anti-Harassment.
- Data Privacy fundamentals.

Training is assigned automatically in the Learning Portal. Completion is tracked, and incomplete training after 30 days is escalated to the manager.

## Probation Period

New employees serve a **90-day probation period**. During this time, the manager provides regular feedback, with a formal check-in at 30, 60, and 90 days. Successful completion is confirmed in writing by the manager and People Ops.

## Getting Paid and Benefits

Payroll runs on the last business day of each month. New hires should review the Benefits Policy and complete benefits enrollment **within 30 days** of their start date. Direct deposit details must be entered in the People Portal during the first week.

## Key Contacts

- People Ops: people-ops@northwind.example
- IT Service Desk: it-help@northwind.example
- Payroll: payroll@northwind.example

## First 90 Days

By the end of your first 90 days you should have completed all mandatory training, set initial goals with your manager, and become familiar with your team's tools and processes. Questions about onboarding can be directed to People Ops at any time.
