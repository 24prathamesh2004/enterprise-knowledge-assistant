# Remote Work Policy

Policy owner: Workplace Experience (David Chen, david.chen@northwind.example)
Effective date: 2026-02-01 | Version 2.4

## Purpose

This policy sets expectations for hybrid and remote working arrangements at Northwind Technologies. It aims to balance flexibility with collaboration, security, and team cohesion.

## Eligibility

Employees become eligible for remote work after completing their first **90 days of employment**. Eligibility also requires a satisfactory performance standing and a role deemed remote-compatible by the department head. Roles requiring on-site presence (lab, facilities, hardware) may be excluded and will be flagged in the job description.

## Hybrid Schedule

Eligible employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**, with a minimum of **2 days per week on-site**. Teams may designate one shared in-office "anchor day" to support collaboration; the default company-wide anchor day is **Wednesday**.

### Core Collaboration Hours

All employees, regardless of location, must be reachable during **core hours of 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM** in their registered local time zone. Outside core hours, employees may structure their schedules flexibly provided they meet the standard 40-hour work week.

## Equipment and Stipend

Northwind provides each remote-eligible employee with a **$500 annual home-office stipend** for peripherals, furniture, and connectivity. The stipend resets each January and is claimed through the Expense Portal (see the Expense Reimbursement Policy). Company laptops remain Northwind property and must be returned upon separation.

## Security Requirements

Remote workers must:

- Connect through the Northwind VPN when accessing internal systems.
- Use only company-issued devices for handling confidential data.
- Ensure home networks are password protected with WPA2 or higher.
- Comply fully with the IT Security Policy, including MFA and screen-lock requirements.

## Fully Remote Arrangements

Fully remote arrangements (0 mandatory on-site days) are exceptional and require **VP-level approval**. Requests are reviewed quarterly and reassessed annually. Fully remote employees must attend at least **one on-site team gathering per quarter**, with travel funded under the Travel Policy.

## Requesting Remote Work

Submit a Remote Work Agreement through the People Portal. The approval chain is: employee → direct manager → department head. Approved arrangements are reviewed every 12 months. Managers may temporarily suspend remote privileges with **5 business days notice** if business needs require on-site presence.

## Relocation

Employees who wish to relocate to a different state or country while remote must notify People Ops at least **60 days in advance**, as this may affect payroll, tax, and legal compliance. Not all locations can be supported.

## Non-Compliance

Repeated failure to meet core-hours availability or on-site attendance expectations may result in revocation of remote privileges. Questions should be directed to Workplace Experience at workplace@northwind.example.
