# Travel Policy

Policy owner: Finance, Travel Desk (Kevin Zhao, kevin.zhao@northwind.example)
Effective date: 2026-02-15 | Version 3.3

## Purpose

This policy governs business travel for Northwind Technologies employees, balancing cost control with traveler comfort and safety. It works alongside the Expense Reimbursement Policy.

## Booking and Approvals

All business travel must be approved by the employee's manager **before booking**. Flights and hotels must be booked through the Northwind Travel Portal at least **14 days in advance** to secure preferred rates. Bookings made with less than 14 days notice require Director approval.

## Air Travel

- **Economy class** is standard for all flights **under 6 hours**.
- **Business class** is permitted for flights **6 hours or longer**.
- Seat upgrades beyond policy are at the traveler's personal expense.

Employees should select the lowest logical fare within a 3-hour window of preferred departure times.

## Per Diem Allowances

Meals and incidentals during travel are covered by a per diem rather than itemized receipts:

- **Domestic travel:** **$65 per day**.
- **International travel:** **$90 per day**.

The per diem is prorated at 75% for departure and return travel days. Per diem replaces the standard domestic meal cap in the Expense Reimbursement Policy while an employee is traveling.

## Lodging

Hotel costs are capped at **$250 per night** for domestic travel and **$300 per night** for international travel, excluding taxes. Costs above the cap require Director approval and written justification. Employees should book standard rooms at approved or preferred hotels where available.

## Ground Transportation

- Use ride-share, taxis, or public transit for local travel.
- Rental cars require manager approval; economy or mid-size class only.
- Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at **$0.62 per mile** per the Expense Policy.

## International Travel

International trips require Director approval and must be registered with the Travel Desk at least **21 days in advance** for visa and safety coordination. Employees traveling internationally are enrolled in the company travel-assistance program automatically.

## Travel Safety

Employees must register itineraries in the Travel Portal so the company can locate and assist travelers in an emergency. Travel to destinations under a government advisory is prohibited without VP approval.

## Expense Submission

Travel expenses are submitted through the Expense Portal **within 30 days** of trip completion, following the Expense Reimbursement Policy. Per diem days do not require meal receipts, but lodging and transport receipts are required.

## Questions

Contact the Travel Desk at travel@northwind.example for booking support or policy exceptions.
